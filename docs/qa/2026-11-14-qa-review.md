## Fixed in this pass

### Player
- **A dropped connection skipped up to 3 songs, then stopped**, losing the place in the song (`engine.ts`). Network errors (Media3 2000–2002, web `MEDIA_ERR_NETWORK`, the 20 s load timeout) now keep the current song, show "…Retrying", and reload from the same position with backoff (2 → 30 s, plus right away when the network comes back; gives up after 10 min). Verified on the emulator: with the server stopped the player stayed on the song, and it resumed by itself when the server came back.
- Repeat-all with a one-song queue played once and stopped.
- After a fast Next, a queue edit could delete the slot now playing (`syncWindow` used a stale `natCur`).
- A seek made right after Next was dropped.

### Battery / performance
- **The star themes redrew the whole window every frame while idle**, including the glass blur: 27 endless Reanimated loops. Twinkles and meteors now share a 10 s cycle with a still gap; idle frames dropped about 5.5× on the emulator.
- Imports reloaded the whole server catalog at startup for every followed job, then on every 2.5 s poll. Now: no reload at startup, at most one every 10 s.
- Android Auto rebuilt and re-wrote its browse tree on every launch and after every song. Now it waits 5 s after start, skips unchanged snapshots, and batches recents-only changes (60 s).
- Rails tracked scroll position on Android (a re-render about 15 times a second while swiping); now only the desktop arrows do.
- The lyrics cache grew forever (on web it shares a ~5 MB localStorage quota). It's now capped at 300 entries on web and 3000 native.

### UX
- **New: Library → Songs** lists every server song A–Z, with Play all and Shuffle. Before this there was no way to browse all songs except through albums or search.
- Library → Albums said "No albums yet" while loading or offline. It now shows a spinner, or "Can't reach your server" with Try again.
- After a reload or a deep link, close/back on the player, lyrics and not-found pages did nothing. They now fall back to Home (one `goBack()` everywhere).
- Lyrics: "Couldn't load lyrics" now has Try again. The player title wraps to two lines.
- A shared playlist could be saved twice with a double tap.
- Turning on "Wi-Fi only" didn't stop downloads already running on mobile data.

### Accessibility
- Daylight theme: the Search browse tiles had near-black text on dark gradients, and the floating back button had a dark icon on a dark scrim. The queue's swipe underlay used white on the accent colour (unreadable with the yellow/green accents).
- Settings switches had no names ("switch, on"); inputs relied on placeholders.
- Bottom sheets now act as modal dialogs for screen readers. Toasts are announced on Android. Filter chips have a bigger touch area.
- Desktop shortcuts: arrow keys go to a focused slider instead of also seeking or changing the volume, and keyboard focus isn't blurred after every shortcut.

### Server
- `GET /<any Cyrillic path>` returned **500** because the ETag was built from the raw path. ETags are now hashed and sanitised.
- `/assets/…` and `/_expo/…` misses returned the app shell with a **year-long immutable** cache header. They now return 404.
- A track deleted during a rescan came back as a ghost row (its stream 404s).
- A deleted import job came back after a restart, because the cancelled run saved it again.
- Random bytes could be indexed as a 21 ms "track". Every scanned file now needs ≥ 0.1 s of audio and a sane bitrate.
- An undecodable embedded cover returned a 500 on every request. It's now a 404, remembered until the track changes.
- Artwork ETags are per size bucket, so `size=100` and `size=128` share one cache entry.
- Upload names sent percent-encoded are stored readable. yt-dlp gets `--` before user URLs. A startup warning appears for tokens under 16 characters (prod's are 32).
- Docs: `/api/library` documented; `private` cache headers when `LISTEN_TOKEN` is set; recents are recorded after 3 s, not 10 s.

New server tests cover the web fixes (101 tests pass).

## Not fixed yet: plan

Ordered by value. None of these blocks daily use at the current library size (~220 songs).

1. **iPhone: first Play after reopening may need a second tap** (plausible, untestable without a real iPhone). The saved position makes the engine wait for `canplay` + seek before `play()`, so the tap's user gesture is gone. Plan: in `audio.web.ts`, call `el.play()` inside the tap and apply the start position on `loadedmetadata`. Then test on the phone from the home screen.
2. **Native playlist window of 200 tracks.** Every jump or edit does about 200 blocking main-thread calls, and each finished download of an upcoming track rebuilds the tail (the notification flickers). Plan: keep 3–5 tracks ahead and top up on each advance; replace a single slot instead of rebuilding.
3. **Persisted queue is full track JSON**, parsed before the first frame and re-written after every edit. Plan: store keys plus the minimum fields (or SQLite rows), and restore after first paint.
4. **Downloads at scale.** Every progress tick copies the whole items map, and every completion re-renders every track list. Plan: a separate progress store keyed by track, debounced persistence, and batched `setDownload` updates.
5. **Library sync reads outside its write transaction.** A rare multi-device lost update. Plan: read, merge and apply inside one exclusive transaction.
6. **Server recomputes all albums and artists on every write.** 15 ms now, about 1 s at 20k tracks, once per imported song. Plan: recompute only the affected albums and artists, or once per import job.
7. **Import job documents are rewritten 3× per song.** About 2 GB of writes for a 1000-song import. Plan: throttle saves or store items as rows.
8. **Web bundle is 3.8 MB.** Plan: check what's in `__common` (icon set, QR, tab-view), lazy-load admin/import/upload, and serve precompressed files from the origin for LAN use.
9. **Smaller UX items:**
   - Queue reorder can't auto-scroll past one screen.
   - No undo for removing from a playlist or the queue, and no confirmation when cancelling an import.
   - Buttons nested inside rows can't be reached with VoiceOver on the web app.
   - With the player open, the screen behind it stays in the TalkBack tree.
   - The queue list's accessibility text reads in reverse order.
10. **Re-run the web pass:** axe-core in both themes, a 4× CPU throttle profile of scrolling and tab switching, and a heap check over 30 album→back loops.

## Follow-up 2026-11-16

Items 1–10 above were worked through. Checked: mobile typecheck + lint, 108 server tests, web export, and on the legion emulator (locked-screen auto-advance, rapid Next ×8 in the background, a download finishing for the next song, queue restore after the upgrade, queue Undo).

- Done: 1 (play() inside the tap; web 502s retried), 2 (native window = current + 5 ahead, topped up on advance, single-slot replace), 3 (compact v2 queue, debounced, lazy restore, v1 migrated), 4 (per-track progress store, batched completions), 5 (the race was in the app: sync now merges inside one write transaction), 7 (import progress saves ≤ 1/s).
- 6: imports rebuild albums/artists at most every 5 s plus once at the end, not per song. A partial recompute wasn't safe because artist splitting depends on every track.
- 8: `__common` 3.87 → 1.52 MB (lucide icons imported per file, QR lazy). Startup JS 5.27 → 2.92 MB.
- 9: queue drag auto-scroll, Undo for queue/playlist removal, confirm on cancelling an import, no nested buttons on web rows, screen behind the player hidden from screen readers. The "reverse reading order" seen in the test tool's list summary also shows on the Songs list, so it is probably the tool, not TalkBack; unconfirmed.
- 10: secondary text contrast raised to 4.5:1, seek slider value exposed, covers marked decorative. No heap leak over 30 album→back loops.
- Still open: white on the red accent is 4.23:1 (design call); missing page landmarks on web; Reanimated is 650 KB of the web bundle; in a burst of fast background Next presses one press can be dropped.
